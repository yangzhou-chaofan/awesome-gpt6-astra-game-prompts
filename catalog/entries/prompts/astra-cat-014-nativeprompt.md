# nativeprompt

## What it is

Rewrites a prompt to the rules of whichever model you are working in — GPT-6 Astra, Claude Fable 5.1, Opus 5, Codex.

## Why it's recommended

Cross-model portability is the practical gap most prompt lists ignore.
